Clear Answers

Software engineering and engagement standards

Direct responses to the questions most frequently raised during enterprise procurement and architectural diligence.

Who owns the source code, intellectual property, and documentation? expand_more
You own 100% of all intellectual property, source repositories, database migrations, Terraform/Kubernetes manifests, and architectural documentation. Full copyright transfers unconditionally upon milestone delivery. We never use proprietary runtime wrappers or restrictive licensing creating vendor lock-in.
How does Tech Titans structure engineering teams and project delivery? expand_more
We operate with dedicated, senior-only engineering squads consisting of a lead systems architect, full-stack engineers, and an SRE/DevOps specialist. All engineers have a minimum of 8 years in continuous production practice. We work in two-week continuous delivery sprints with direct client visibility into staging repositories.
What technology stacks and architectural paradigms do you specialize in? expand_more
We specialize in proven, high-concurrency technologies. Our stack includes TypeScript/Node.js, Go, Python, and C# on the backend. We build web systems with modern TypeScript, and mobile apps with React Native, Swift, and Kotlin. For operational datastores, we implement PostgreSQL and Redis. For cloud infrastructure, we configure Kubernetes, Docker, and Terraform across AWS, GCP, and Azure.
How do you handle enterprise security, data privacy, and compliance? expand_more
Every line of code and infrastructure manifest is written to align with SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and GDPR standards. We implement zero-trust network boundaries, automated security analysis (SAST/DAST) in CI/CD pipelines, end-to-end encryption in transit (mTLS) and at rest (AES-256), and immutable audit logs.
How do you structure project pricing, contracts, and invoicing? expand_more
Depending on project maturity, we provide either fixed-price, deliverable-gated milestones (ideal for defined architectural scoping and initial product builds) or dedicated monthly engineering retainers (ideal for ongoing system scaling, feature velocity, and multi-year SRE stewardship). Invoices are tied strictly to verified deliverables.
What are your post-deployment maintenance SLAs and support response times? expand_more
We offer structured enterprise SLA agreements covering 24/7/365 telemetry monitoring, security patch management, framework upgrades, and incident mitigation. For critical production tier-1 alerts (P1), our guaranteed response time is under 15 minutes with dedicated on-call site reliability engineers.
